Ravenflight Browser – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 (applies to version 1.1.5 and later)
Developer: Jesse Raines
Contact: rainesjesse617@gmail.com
App: Ravenflight (Android package com.ravenflight.browser; desktop app for Linux, Windows and macOS)

Summary

The developer of Ravenflight does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no crash reporting, and it does not send anything to the developer. Your browsing data stays on your device. The one exception is the optional Grok AI assistant: when you use it, your chat messages (and, if you tap “Ask about this page”, the current page's title, address and visible text) are sent directly from your device to xAI using your own API key. If you report an AI reply, the report is kept on your device and is only sent to the developer if you choose to email it. Details are below.

Data stored on your device

To work as a web browser, Ravenflight keeps the following data locally on your device only:

  • Browsing history (page titles and addresses) – not recorded in private tabs; you can delete single entries or clear it all in Settings.
  • Bookmarks you create.
  • Settings – search engine, ad & tracker blocker on/off, the sites where you chose to allow ads, a running count of blocked requests, colour theme, start-page wallpaper, website dark mode, Grok model and similar preferences.
  • Custom wallpaper image – if you pick your own start-page picture, a copy of that one image is kept in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded; choosing another image or uninstalling the app removes it. On Android the image is chosen with the system photo picker, so no storage permission is requested.
  • Your xAI API key (only if you add one) – stored encrypted (Android: EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by the Android Keystore; desktop: the operating system's secure storage via Electron safeStorage). If no secure storage is available, the key is kept in memory for the current session only. You can remove it in Settings at any time.
  • Cookies, cache and site storage created by websites you visit, managed by the system web engine (Android System WebView / Chromium). You can clear these in Settings. Private tabs use a separate session that is discarded when the last private tab is closed.
  • AI content reports (only if you report a Grok reply) – the text of the reported reply, the reason you picked, your optional comment, the time and the model name, kept in a local reports log so you can review them. You can view or clear them in Settings.
  • Downloaded files you choose to download, saved to your device's Downloads folder.
  • Shortcut logos (Android) – small icons for the start-page shortcut sites, kept in the app's cache for up to two weeks. They are never saved from private tabs and are deleted when you clear history or browsing data.
  • Saved news headlines (Android) – the most recent headlines for the news feed (title, link, source name and time) in the app's cache, so the home page loads instantly and works offline. They are replaced about every 15 minutes while you use the feed, are never saved from private tabs, and are deleted when you clear browsing data or uninstall the app.
  • Saved story pictures (Android) – small copies of news-story pictures (and a note when a story has none) in the app’s cache, kept so the feed doesn’t download them again; never saved from private tabs; deleted when you clear browsing data or uninstall the app.
  • Premium theme unlocks (Android) – which premium themes you have bought (their Google Play product IDs), so they stay unlocked offline. This is restored from Google Play each time the app starts.

None of this data is transmitted to the developer (except an AI content report you choose to email yourself, see below). On Android, app data is excluded from cloud backup. Uninstalling the app removes the app's stored data (downloaded files stay in your Downloads folder).

Network connections

Ravenflight connects only to:

  • the websites you choose to visit;
  • the search engine you select when you type a search (Android: Google by default, with DuckDuckGo available in Settings; desktop: DuckDuckGo by default, with Google available);
  • on Android, the start-page shortcut sites themselves (for example wikipedia.org or github.com), to download each site's own logo (its home page and icon files) about once every two weeks. This is never done from private tabs, no cookies are sent, and no third-party favicon service is used;
  • on Android, Google News, when the home page’s news feed is on (it is on by default). The home page downloads headlines from Google News’s public RSS feeds (news.google.com) for your device’s language and country. These requests contain only what any web request contains (your IP address, the app’s user agent and the language/country edition); no cookies, account, browsing history or other personal data are sent, and the developer receives nothing. It is never done in private tabs, and you can turn it off with Settings → Show news feed on home page or Hide feed in the feed’s menu. Ravenflight is not affiliated with Google News;
  • on Android, news sites, to show each story’s picture in the feed. Only for cards that are about to scroll onto the screen, the app (1) asks news.google.com for the address of the original article behind the Google News link, (2) downloads the start of that article page from the news site (only the page header, at most 256 KB, to read its preview-image address, e.g. og:image), and (3) downloads that picture. If there is no picture, the app may download the news site’s own logo instead (as for shortcuts). These requests go directly from your device to Google News and the news site (and the image server the site names), with no cookies and no account; like any web request they reveal your IP address and the app’s user agent, and the site may learn that the story was shown in your feed. Nothing is sent to the developer and no third-party service is used. Pictures are shrunk and cached on your device only (memory and app cache), are deleted when you clear browsing data or uninstall the app, and are never fetched in private tabs or when the feed is off. Tapping a story opens it in the browser like any link.
  • Google Play, only on Android and only for premium themes (see below).
  • xAI's API (api.x.ai) – only if you use the optional Grok assistant.

Websites and search engines receive the information browsers normally send (for example your IP address, browser user agent and the requested page) and are governed by their own privacy policies, e.g. DuckDuckGo and Google.

The built-in ad & tracker blocker works entirely on your device using a list of domains bundled with the app (the list is not downloaded or updated from any server). No browsing data is sent anywhere to perform blocking, and blocked-request counts are kept only on your device. Website dark mode, themes and wallpapers are likewise applied locally and do not contact any server.

Premium themes and Google Play (Android)

Premium themes are optional one-time purchases made through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the developer never receives your payment details. To show prices, complete purchases and restore them, the app talks to the Google Play app on your device, which shares your purchase history for Ravenflight's products (product ID, purchase status and a purchase token) with the app. The app keeps only the list of unlocked themes on your device; it is not sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. Google's handling of purchases is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

VPN shortcut (Android)

The “VPN” entry in the menu and Settings only opens the separate Proton VPN app if it is installed, or its Google Play page if it is not. Ravenflight does not contain a VPN, does not send any data to Proton and is not affiliated with Proton. To show whether a VPN is on, the app checks the device's network state locally.

Grok AI assistant (optional)

Ravenflight includes an optional chat assistant powered by xAI's Grok API. It does nothing until you add your own xAI API key in Settings, and it only sends data when you actively use the chat:

  • Chat messages: when you send a message, the text of the current conversation (your messages and Grok's earlier replies in this session) is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection directly from your device to xAI (api.x.ai), authenticated with your API key.
  • “Ask about this page”: only when you tap this button, the current page's title, address (URL) and visible text (truncated to about 12,000 characters) are sent to xAI together with your question. In private tabs this is turned off by default (you can allow it in Settings).
  • Nothing is sent to xAI in the background, and nothing is ever sent to the developer. The developer cannot see your conversations.
  • Inside the app the conversation is kept in memory only. It is discarded when you tap Clear chat or close the app.
  • Reporting replies: you can flag any Grok reply as offensive, harmful or inaccurate (flag icon or long-press → Report). The reported reply is hidden and the report (reply text, reason, optional comment, time, model) is stored locally on your device only. Nothing is sent automatically. Only if you tap “Send report to developer” does Ravenflight open your own email app with the report filled in, addressed to the developer; it is sent only if you send that email yourself, and the developer then receives it together with your email address. Reports are used only to investigate and address problematic AI output and are not shared with anyone else. Reports are not sent to xAI.

Data you send to xAI is processed by xAI under its own terms and privacy policy (x.ai/legal/privacy-policy), including how long xAI retains API requests. Usage is billed to your own xAI account. Please do not send sensitive personal information to the assistant. AI-generated replies may be inaccurate.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet / network state – to load web pages (and to reach xAI if you use the assistant), and to show whether a VPN is active.
  • Google Play billing – added by the Google Play Billing library so you can buy premium themes.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save files you explicitly download.
  • Notifications – used by the system download manager to show download progress.

Ravenflight does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or SMS permissions. Websites may ask for access to things like your camera or files through the standard system prompts; that access is only granted if you allow it.

Security

Data sent to xAI travels over HTTPS (TLS). Your API key is stored with the platform's encrypted storage as described above. Because the developer does not operate any server for Ravenflight, there is no developer-held copy of your data that could be exposed.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Clear history, cookies, cache and site data in Settings.
  • Turn off the home-page news feed in Settings (Show news feed on home page) to stop all requests to Google News and to news sites for story pictures.
  • Use private tabs to browse without saving history.
  • Use Clear chat to erase the assistant conversation; remove your API key in Settings to switch the assistant off.
  • View or clear your AI content reports in Settings. To have an emailed report deleted, email the developer at the address below.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.
  • For data already sent to xAI, use the controls and contact options described in xAI's privacy policy.

Children

Ravenflight is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
