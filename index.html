Ravenflight Browser

A fast, private web browser with a midnight-dark raven look.

Ravenflight keeps your browsing on your device, blocks the ads and trackers that slow pages down, and lets you style the browser your way. No account, no sign-up, and no analytics.

What you get

Ad & tracker blocker

Blocks about 350 known ad and tracking domains, right on your device. Tap the shield to allow ads on sites you want to support.

News on your home page

Headline cards with each story’s own picture, below your shortcuts. You can turn the feed off in Settings.

Private tabs

Browse without saving history. Private tabs wipe their cookies when you close them.

Dark mode for websites

Darken bright pages: Off, On, or Follow system.

Raven themes and wallpapers

Four free themes, six premium raven themes ($0.99 each), night-sky wallpapers, or your own photo.

Grok AI assistant (optional)

Chat with Grok or ask about the page you’re reading, using your own xAI key. Nothing is sent unless you use it.

Easy VPN access

Open the free Proton VPN app straight from the menu.
