Changelog

What’s new in Ravenflight, newest first. Versions 1.1.3 and 1.1.4 were folded into 1.1.5 and were not published on their own.

1.1.15 for Linux Nov 16, 2026

  • The Linux app catches up with Android 1.1.15.
  • Google is the default search. If you never chose one, an older DuckDuckGo default moves to Google. A choice you already made stays.
  • Gemini is the default AI assistant, and Grok is still an option. You bring your own key, and it is stored securely on your computer.
  • The start page can show a Google News feed, with each story’s picture. Turn it off in Settings. It never appears in private tabs.
  • One top bar: back, forward, home, the address field, reload, the AI button, and the menu. There is no bottom bar.
  • A present for GNU/FOSS: on Linux, all six premium raven themes are free. A one-time note explains the gift, a penguin sits beside it on the theme picker, and each premium theme is marked free on Linux. On Windows and macOS those themes are listed as coming soon. The penguin is Larry Ewing’s Tux, with thanks to lewing@isc.tamu.edu and the GIMP.
  • Linux downloads are an AppImage and a .deb, with a snap as well.

1.1.15 for Android Nov 14, 2026

  • Gilded Raven is free through April 1, 2027 (your phone’s local time). Settings shows “Free until Apr 1, 2027” instead of a price while the offer lasts. From April 2, 2027 it locks again unless you bought it or redeemed a code. A purchase or redeemed code keeps the theme for good. If you are still using Gilded when the offer ends and you don’t own it, the browser returns to the default theme.
  • Pages that are not encrypted are marked Not secure in the address bar. News and site icons are loaded only over HTTPS. Your AI keys stay encrypted on the device, cloud backup stays off, and moving to a new phone does not copy the app’s saved settings or files.

1.1.14 for Android Nov 14, 2026

  • Premium themes can be bought in the app: Gilded Raven, Blood Moon Raven, Midnight Murder, Frost Raven, Ember Raven, and Emerald Omen. Buying one unlocks that theme, and purchases are restored when you open the app.
  • Redeem a promo code from the theme screen. Google Play applies the code. A code you redeem outside the app is picked up the next time you open Ravenflight.

1.1.13 for Android Nov 10, 2026

  • Google Gemini is the default AI assistant, with xAI Grok as an option. Choose either one in Settings.
  • Add your own Gemini key or your own Grok key. Both are stored encrypted on your device.
  • Chat, ask about the page you are reading, clear, stop, and report all work with whichever assistant you picked. The panel title follows that choice.
  • If you never chose an assistant, you start on Gemini. A Grok key you already saved is kept, and switching back to Grok uses it right away.

1.1.12 for Android Nov 2, 2026

  • Premium theme purchases are checked with Google Play before a theme unlocks.

1.1.11 for Android Oct 31, 2026

  • Tighter protection while you browse. Risky links opened by a web page are blocked, mixed insecure content is blocked, and the address bar only accepts normal web addresses.
  • Downloads can’t disguise their file names, and downloaded apps are not opened automatically.
  • A site must ask before it uses the camera or microphone. Google’s Safe Browsing stays on. The web engine itself still updates through Google Play.

1.1.10 for Android Oct 30, 2026

  • Tapping a bookmark or a history item opens that page.
  • Bookmarks and History in Settings open the same lists.

1.1.9 for Android Oct 30, 2026

  • Add a bookmark from the Bookmarks screen, with a name and address. Saving a page you already bookmarked updates its name. Long-press a bookmark to edit or delete it.
  • Bookmark this page from the menu still works.

1.1.8 for Android Oct 30, 2026

  • The tab switcher sits clear of the status bar, so its buttons are easy to tap. Each card shows the page picture on top and the title underneath.

1.1.7 for Android Oct 30, 2026

  • The tab button opens a full-screen grid of your tabs. Each card shows a picture of the page, its icon, the title, and a close button. Swipe a card sideways to close it.
  • From the switcher you can close all tabs, open a new tab, open a private tab, or tap Done. Private tabs are marked. New tab and Private tab in the menu still work.

1.1.6 for Android Oct 26, 2026

  • Links opened from other apps now load in a tab, instead of a blank start page.
  • If another app shares text, Ravenflight opens the first web address in it. If there is no address, it searches with your default engine. Only normal web links are opened from other apps.

1.1.5 for Android Oct 23, 2026

Includes 1.1.3 and 1.1.4, which were folded into this release and were not published on their own.

  • News cards lead with a picture of the story. Pictures load as you scroll to them, and never in private tabs. If a story has no picture, you see the site’s logo or a letter tile.

1.1.4 for Android Oct 23, 2026

Folded into 1.1.5. Not published on its own. It also includes 1.1.3.

  • One top toolbar. Back, forward, and home sit beside the address field, then the tab button and the menu. The bottom bar is gone. On a narrow screen, forward moves into the menu. Long-press back to see history.
  • A news feed on the home page, from Google News, with topic chips, pull to refresh, and more stories as you scroll. It never loads in private tabs. Turn it off in Settings or from the feed’s own menu. The credit reads “via Google News.”

1.1.3 for Android Oct 23, 2026

Folded into 1.1.5. Not published on its own.

  • Six premium raven themes, $0.99 each: Midnight Murder, Blood Moon Raven, Gilded Raven, Frost Raven, Ember Raven, and Emerald Omen. You can preview a locked theme before you buy. Purchases can be restored from Settings.
  • Google is the default search on Android. DuckDuckGo is still available. A choice you already made is kept.
  • A new menu with a default-browser prompt, quick actions, themes, the AI assistant, find, share, new and private tabs, VPN, and Settings.
  • Shortcut tiles use each site’s own logo, or a letter if none is available. Logos are not saved from private tabs.
  • A Proton VPN shortcut in the menu and in Settings. It opens the Proton VPN app, or the Play page if you don’t have it, and shows whether a VPN is on. Ravenflight is not affiliated with Proton.

1.1.1 for Android and desktop Oct 20, 2026

  • Report an AI reply from the flag under it, or by long-pressing on Android or right-clicking on desktop. Pick a reason and add an optional comment. The reply collapses to “You reported this reply,” and you can show it again.
  • Reports stay on your device. Send report to developer opens your email app with the details filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email.
  • Settings lists those reports and can clear them.
  • The chat notes that AI replies can be wrong or offensive, and that you can tap the flag to report one.

1.1.0 for Android and desktop Oct 19, 2026

  • The ad and tracker blocker covers more sites, about 350. A badge on the shield shows how many were blocked on the page. Tap the shield to allow ads on that site, and reset the list in Settings.
  • Dark mode for websites: Off, On, or Follow system.
  • Raven themes for the browser: Midnight Raven, Crimson Raven, Storm Grey, and Emerald Night.
  • Start-page wallpapers: four night-sky pictures, or your own photo.
  • Optional Grok assistant, using your own key: chat, clear the chat, and ask about the page you are reading. Asking about the page is off in private tabs unless you turn it on.

1.0.0 Oct 19, 2026

  • First release: tabs, private tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads, a start page, a choice of search engine, the built-in blocker, and settings.
